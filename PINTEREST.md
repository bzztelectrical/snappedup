## How to make and post pins
- **Image:** `pinterest/pin.py <pins.json>` → 1000×1500 JPG: yellow kicker pill, big white title on red, white panel with 1–3 product photos (or "big" text), and a black footer with snappedupdeals.co.uk. Product photos as in TIKTOK.md (Chrome zoom save_to_disk at scale 1; never Amazon images).
- **Link pins to our guides** (`https://snappedupdeals.co.uk/guides/<slug>/`). The guide pages carry the Amazon/Awin links and the disclosure, and their prices stay current. Only use direct Amazon links (`?tag=snappedup-21`) for a deal with a price checked that day, and say "affiliate link" in the description.
- **Every description** ends "Contains affiliate links: we may earn commission."
- **Flow:** commit the image to the laptop → stage → pinterest.co.uk/pin-creation-tool/ → File Upload → Title (keyword-first) → Description → Link → Board → Publish. Pinterest can also schedule pins ("publish later"), which the weekly batch should use.
- **Cadence:** Pinterest rewards steady daily pinning over bursts. Aim for 3–5 pins a day, including new pins for the same guide with a different image or title.
